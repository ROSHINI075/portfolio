ScamShield AI Architecture

NLP Text Classification

Applies TF-IDF vectorization and machine learning models (Random Forest / Logistic Regression) trained to differentiate scam vocabulary from legitimate corporate job offers.

Financial Extortion Heuristics

Detects requests for "security deposits", "registration fees", or "training kits" disguised as employment prerequisites.

Channel Verification

Flags non-corporate communication channels such as unsolicited Telegram or WhatsApp recruitment messages lacking verifiable domain records.

Resume ATS Optimizer

Compares candidate resume skill keywords against verified industry descriptions to ensure high ATS compatibility and legitimate placement.
